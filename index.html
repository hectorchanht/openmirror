OpenMirror

See yourself clearly — through your own behaviour.

Give the mirror your own records, and it returns a grounded reading of who you are: how you love, how you work, how you learn, your arcs and your blind spots.

Status: Founding — initialized, first release in preparation

What it is

After an AI reads a person's own behavioural record, it can produce a reading of who that person is — potentially clearer than the person's own self-knowledge. OpenMirror is that product: give it your own records — messages, calendars, journals, photos — and it returns a grounded reading. Its iron rules: local-first, your own copy, never a cloud honeypot; every claim cites the behaviour it rests on, interpretation labelled as interpretation; consent is the gate — a person scans only their own records. The mirror serves the person: 互相影響，互相幫助 — you see yourself clearer and act better.

END GOAL

A mirror that serves the person: everyone who gives it their own records sees themselves clearer and acts better — and the AI learns how to help that specific person.
